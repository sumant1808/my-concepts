## Verified OK

- **Counts [SRC, script]:** 8 layers, 51 groups, 114 components (C69, C82 and C88 are unused IDs), 21 externals, 60 links and 44 standards. The xref per-layer `components`, `groups` and `counts` (BIAN, FIX, FpML/CDM, ISO 20022, MT, regs) match the components exactly for all 8 layers. The coverage totals (BIAN 67, FIX 23, FpML/CDM 18, ISO 20022 25, MT 12, regs 45) match. by_band (B1 2/12/22, B2 1/7/12, B3 2/12/26, B4 2/13/32, B5 1/7/22) matches. coverage.md before/after table matches.
- **Links [SRC]:** every endpoint is a real component or external, and every from_layer and to_layer matches where the component sits. Each external's layers include the layers it links to.
- **Stage traceability [SRC]:** every group listed exists with the stated layer and name, every group that carries a stage tag is listed under that stage, and layer stage sets equal the union of their groups' stages. No old group names appear anywhere in spec.json (grep for Structuring and Suitability, Daily Risk, Collateral, Liquidity and Cash, Reconciliation Services: none, apart from the generic phrase "collateral, liquidity and cash" in prose).
- **BIAN, new labels [SRC, v10 HTML parsed for «ServiceDomain»]:** Customer Case Management (C104), Dealer Desk (C106), Quant Model (C108) and Contribution Analysis (C116) are all verbatim service domains. All other BIAN labels are verbatim service domains except C78 "Market Risk" (#13). C74 "Financial Instrument Reference Data Management" is verbatim; it wraps across two lines in the SVG.
- **CDM eligible collateral on C112 [SRC]:** reasonable. CDM docs reference "standardised eligible collateral schedules" and an "eligible-collateral-representation" page. That haircuts and concentration limits are in the representation is [OWN].
- **Regs real and correctly named:** PRA SS1/23 model risk management principles for banks [SRC: BoE/PRA publication and PRA rulebook entry dated 17-05-2024 found]. US SR 11-7, MiFID II Art 16(7) recording, UMR for eligibility and haircuts, Basel SA-CCR, Basel CVA risk framework, UK stamp duty and SDRT, US IRC 871(m) dividend equivalents, and EU PRIIPs KID scoped to packaged retail products [OWN].
