Method and coverage

Source: your X Following tab, read-only in Chrome (scroll and DOM read only). Window start reached (feed depth 2026-11-15 21:30Z). Raw captured 2976; in window 2849; original posts 2410 (reposts excluded: they carry the original date); relevance filter kept 574; exported 395 from 37 authors; 179 skipped by per-author caps (ranked by cashtags, keywords, views). Text truncated to ~230 chars at export. Weekend coverage ends Sat 21 Nov ~15:30 UTC.
Scoring. Author weight = tier weight x (0.5 + 0.5 x (credentials + track record)/6). One vote per author per ticker; stance = sum of direction x conviction factor (0.25/0.5/0.8/1.0), clipped to [-1,1]; promo posts count half; engagement is not scored. Score = sum(weight x stance). Listed names need 2+ authors; "meets full threshold" needs 3+ authors and one analyst/top investor.
Verification. First-pass Jev extraction on multi-ticker posts was noisy, so the final ranking uses only votes that were re-scored by Jev and kept only if the post was about that ticker with a bullish or bearish view (32 re-scored votes dropped for being off-ticker or having no directional view). 28 first-pass directional votes were never re-scored and are not counted, so names such as NVDA may have extra unscored views. Limitation: for 5 of the 9 listed names, one of the two votes is a single list-style post from JasonL_Capital ("best stocks in every sector"), so those names are thin. Excluded outright: PS (Ackman's own vehicle), GS (an S&P target, not a view on GS), SAGCV (non-US), KEEL (unresolved), SOXL and SPY (leveraged/index products). Flags: "finfluencer-only" = no analyst or top investor among voters; "low confidence" = a kept vote had Jev confidence under 0.6.
Data gaps. 2y/10y yield for Fri 20 Nov unavailable (FRED lag); week measured 13->19 Nov.; WTI spot only to 17 Nov (FRED lag); USO used as proxy for week.; Gold/silver spot only a Saturday snapshot (XAU 3535.45, XAG 41.17); no daily history (AV GOLD_SILVER_HISTORY returned empty). GLD/SLV used for week move; spot level seems inconsistent with GLD scale - treat with caution.; DXY not available; UUP used as proxy (UUP flat +0.41%).; ANT has no 13 Nov close (listed 19 Nov); IPO price 83 taken from task brief, not verified in data.; OAI: not found. SPCX, BMNR, STRC resolved.; IPO calendar next week: no data.; SNDK Friday change shown blank in LatestClosingPrices; computed from daily history (-16.06%).; No FOMC/Fed speaker or minutes dates from feed (FOMC minutes typically released ~3 weeks post meeting; not verified).
