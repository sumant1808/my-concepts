UC-01 · Implement · Monitor & report

Agentic concierge: one bond maturity, three lanes

The concierge watches the book, prepares the work and reaches out, with the relationship manager approving what matters. Client, concierge agent and RM share one story from Monday trigger to Friday close.

Press → to start the walkthrough
Actors
Client (app, voice or email) · Concierge agent (orchestrator routing to KYC, planning, portfolio, ops, reporting and compliance agents) · Relationship manager
Value evidence
AI can lift RM productivity by up to 45% [2]. Advisors spend 41% of time on back office vs 23% with clients [10]. Proactive nudges follow KPMG agent examples [10].
Control layer
Permissions · human approvals · audit log · explainability. AI disclosure (EU AI Act, from 2 Aug 2026) [19]. SM&CR accountable person recorded [14].

UC-03 · Plan & advise · Compliance & risk

Compliance in the room: the copilot flags it as it happens

The copilot checks the conversation against disclosure, suitability and Consumer Duty expectations. Green means evidenced, not assumed. The compliance officer's phone only lights up when something is escalated.

Press → to start the walkthrough
Actors
Adviser with compliance copilot · Client · Group compliance officer (mobile, escalations only)
Why it matters
Enforcement punishes what a firm cannot evidence: SJP set aside £426m (Feb 2024) to refund ongoing-advice fees without evidence of service [25]; earlier FSA action centred on record-keeping [24].
Built-in checks
Timestamped disclosure evidence · generic-rationale detection · interests and conflicts check · understanding check · vulnerability prompt · fee and value disclosure.
