Wealth is growing.
Margins are not.

Generative AI answers when asked. Agentic AI acts proactively: it watches the book, prepares the work and reaches out, with a person approving what matters. Deloitte describes it as a network of agents, for example across KYC maintenance and AML investigations, that needs strong governance [21].

Stress test: what enforcement actually punishes

We checked the compliance scenario against what the regulator has acted on at St James’s Place. The pattern is less “a missing disclosure sentence” and more “the firm could not evidence what it promised and charged for”.

Paid for, not evidenced

From February 2024 SJP set aside £426m to refund clients who paid ongoing advice fees without evidence of the service being delivered. Record gaps before 2021 were part of it [25]. It later revised its redress method and released £84.5m of the provision [26].

Now built in: an ongoing service schedule created in the session, and a portal view of reviews evidenced.

Records and monitoring

Earlier FSA enforcement against SJP companies (£250,000 in one notice) centred on record-keeping and weak monitoring of replacement sales. Suitability letters had been approved despite deficiencies [24].

Now built in: evidence with timestamps, a check for generic rationale wording, and QA sampling of “green” sessions.

Undisclosed interests

Separately, the FCA fined a former SJP partner £300,000 and banned him for misleading clients, including about his own interest in investments and by implying SJP endorsed them. That was the individual, not the firm [27].

Now built in: interests and conflicts are a named disclosure check, confirmed by the adviser.

Consumer Duty outcomes

The Duty looks at outcomes such as price and value, understanding and support, not just whether a document was sent. Scrutiny of ongoing-advice fees is explicitly tied to it [25].

Now built in: an understanding check, a vulnerability prompt, and fee and value disclosure.

What I could not confirm

I found no FCA fine on SJP for the ongoing-advice issue. What is on record is a redress provision, plus older penalties for record-keeping and an individual’s fine for undisclosed interests. The cited case sources are mostly secondary (claims firms, press); the FCA notices are primary.
