What stalls agents

Not the model.

NatWest and UOB practitioners report that unclear processes, weak data and governance gaps now constrain agentic AI more than the technology does.

What scales them

An operating model.

Shared platform, reusable patterns, controls by default, trained people and measured value, owned by the domains that benefit.

Three structures. One fits now.

Central build factory

Works for the first few use cases. Then the hub becomes the bottleneck and domains never own outcomes.

Rejected
Hub and domain

A shared platform exists and domains have delivery capacity. Fails only without graduation criteria.

Recommended
Fully federated

Right for mature, distinct domains. Too early, and it creates duplicated agents and inconsistent identity.

Control the output and the action, not the model

T1Low

Internal inform or draft (P03, P06). Paved-road controls, first-line sign-off, 5% sampled QA.

T2Medium

Feeds a credit or financial-crime judgement, or acts with approval. Recompute in code, human gate, second-line reviewed pack, 10–20% QA to start.

Not in scopeT3High

Autonomous action on clients, money, credit or regulatory reporting. Not permitted until a tier 3 standard exists.

Output

Accredited reviewer sees evidence; overrides captured.

After

Online evals, sampled QA, drift alerts, kill switch.

Sampling rates are starting points for second line to own. AWS grounding checks do not cover chat conversations; Automated Reasoning checks are English-only and non-streaming.

Operational resilience

An agent is a resource, not a service.

Map it to the important business service it supports. Scale it only when that service stays within impact tolerance with the agent switched off.

Six rules for every agent

  • Map agent, model, tools and region to the service
  • Keep the manual route and the skills behind it
  • Kill switch per agent, per pattern, platform-wide
  • Degrade: alternate model, read-only, human queue
  • Test outages, silent degradation, poisoned documents, cost runaway
  • Log incidents against the service before the 18 March 2027 reporting regime

Usage shows an agent is running.
Baselines show it is working.

Counts as value

  • Improvement against a pre-build baseline
  • Net of run cost and human review time
  • Declared use of released capacity: absorb growth, redeploy or reduce cost
  • Quality and override rates reported alongside

Does not count

  • Vendor benchmark percentages
  • Hours saved with no decision on their use
  • Activity volumes and log-ins
  • Gains without a quality check
Stop rule

Two missed monthly value reviews send an agent back for redesign or retirement. Retiring agents early is the system working.
