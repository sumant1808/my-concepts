Where Asset Classes Diverge
EquitiesCentral clearing via CCPs; T+1 settlement (US, since May 2024); heavy corporate-actions/dividend load in back office.
FXMostly bilateral; settlement risk removed via CLS for eligible pairs; NDFs increasingly clear via LCH ForexClear.
Fixed IncomeRFQ-driven execution, not a central order book; settles via DTCC (US) / Euroclear-Clearstream (Europe).
Structured ProductsHeaviest lift is pre-trade structuring/scenario pricing; booked as OTC derivatives so ISDA/CSA and XVA dominate stages 4-6.

The Other Side

The governance gap is the real constraint on scale.

Autonomy

Agents act, not just predict — they call tools and APIs and chain decisions. Static, point-in-time model review doesn't transfer to systems that act continuously.

Auditability

Audit trails are fragmented — prompts, tool calls, and reasoning steps scatter across systems, making it hard to reconstruct why an agent acted.

Regulation

Frameworks are still immature — no comprehensive regime yet governs agentic AI in finance; supervisors are building as the technology moves.

Human-in-the-loop

Oversight vs. speed is a real trade-off — a human at every step can negate the efficiency case; three-lines-of-defense models are being redesigned for continuous, autonomous risk.
